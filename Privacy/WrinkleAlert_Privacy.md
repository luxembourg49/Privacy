## Collecting, Using and Sharing your Personal and Face Data

Mediclinic does not collect, use or share any personal or face data from any of your use of our App. If we have any of your personal information, we will not willingly sell or give it to anyone, except if lawfully subpoenaed to produce it and then only after offering you a reasonable chance to challenge such subpoena if allowed by law.

### Personal and Face Data

Mediclinic does not collect or use any Personal or Face Data.

### Usage Data

Usage Data is not collected or used when using the Service from Mediclinic. Usage Data is only stored locally in the core Data Framework of your phone and backup using iCloud (if applicable). All the processing and persistence of the data are done on the device. Mediclinic has no access to users' personal data, including facial data, and will not share it with third parties. Users can send data by email to any party with the system default email app. This feature includes a clear indication of the sender and receiver so that the user understands who this data is sent to/shared with.

### Information Collected while Using the Application

While using Our Application, the data will only be collected locally on the core data framework. Before deciding on a wrinkle treatment, please seek advice from your physician.

## Use of your Personal and Face Data

Mediclinic does not collect or use any Personal or Face Data from any of your use of our App.

## Retention of your Personal and Face Data

As Personal and Face Data is not collected, no Personal and Face Data is retained.

### Disclosure of your Personal and Face Data

#### Business Transactions

If the Company is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, no Personal or Face Data will be transferred as no personal data is collected using the application.

#### Law Enforcement

Under no circumstances can Mediclinic be required to disclose your personal or facial data if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities, as personal data is not collected or used through the application.

## Security of your Personal and Face Data

The security of your Personal and Face Data is important to us, but remember Mediclinic doesn’t collect, use, or share any Personal or Face data.
